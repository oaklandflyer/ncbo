> **Read this first.** The requirements below are modelled on the sport club policies that
> universities commonly apply. They are consistent enough across institutions to be a useful
> starting checklist — but **your school's own policy governs you**, not this page. Where the
> two differ, your school wins. Get your institution's current sport club handbook from your
> campus recreation or student activities office and work from that.
>
> NCBO does not certify, insure or supervise any club, and nothing here creates a legal
> obligation or discharges one.

## Officer structure

Four roles cover the functions most sport club programmes expect to see filled:

- **President** — the accountable contact for the university, runs meetings, signs off
  paperwork.
- **Treasurer** — budget, dues, purchases, financial records, fundraising compliance.
- **Event / Game Day Coordinator** — facility reservations, event logistics, visiting clubs.
- **Risk Management Officer** — waivers, emergency action plans, first aid provision,
  incident reports. This is the role clubs most often skip and most need.

Spread these across at least three people. A club where one person holds everything collapses
when that person graduates — which they will, on a known date.

## Annual registration and recertification

- **Register or recertify the club every year.** Missing the deadline typically suspends
  facility access and funding.
- **Officer training** is usually mandatory before the club may operate.
- Keep these **on file and current**: bylaws, roster, budget, and equipment inventory.

## Waivers and emergency contacts

- **Every participant signs a waiver and provides an emergency contact before taking part in
  any activity.** Before the first rep, not at the end of the first session.
- When you host, **visiting athletes sign waivers too**.
- Store them where an officer can retrieve them quickly, and keep them for as long as your
  school requires.

## Medical and first aid

- **At least two officers certified in CPR/AED/First Aid present at club activities.**
- A **stocked first aid kit accessible** at every activity.
- **Accident and incident reports filed within 24 hours.**
- **Medical clearance required before returning to activity after a head injury.** No
  exceptions, no "he says he's fine".

Certification lapses. Put the expiry dates in the officer transition checklist.

## Travel

- **File travel authorisation in advance** of any club trip.
- Observe your school's **driver eligibility rules** — approved drivers, licence checks,
  vehicle rules.
- Observe **restrictions on overnight driving**. Most policies restrict or prohibit driving in
  the small hours, which is exactly when a club driving back from a show wants to.

Plan the return leg before you enter the event. A 9pm finish four hours away is a hotel, not a
drive.

## Hazing — non-negotiable

**Hazing is prohibited.** No exceptions, no traditions, no "it's just what we do".

**What counts:** any act, whether physical, psychological or social, that is required or
expected of someone as a condition of joining or maintaining membership, and that risks harm,
humiliation or degradation — **regardless of whether the person consented**. Consent is not a
defence, because the pressure to consent is the mechanism.

In a bodybuilding context, forms this can take include forced eating or drinking, imposed
training or "initiation" workouts, mandated weight or body-fat requirements, forced posing or
undressing, and sleep or food deprivation.

**Reporting:** report to your university's student conduct office or dean of students, and to
your campus recreation office. Most universities also run an anonymous reporting line — find
your school's and put it in your club's own documents so nobody has to search for it in a
crisis. Report to NCBO as well, at the contact address on the main site, once you have
reported it to the university.

## Fundraising and sponsorship

- **Get fundraising and sponsorship approved** through the required university process before
  committing to anything.
- **Alcohol and tobacco sponsorships are prohibited.** For a physique club, treat sponsorship
  offers from supplement and "wellness" brands with the same caution — see the supplement
  section of the [prep guide](/resources/prep) for why an uncertified product carries risk
  you would be putting your members' eligibility behind.
- **Using university marks** — the name, logo, colours — is restricted and usually requires
  licensing approval. This includes merchandise and Instagram graphics.

## Facilities

- Observe **facility reservation lead times**. They are longer than new officers expect, and
  the good slots go first.
- Have a written **emergency action plan** for each space you use: nearest AED, nearest phone,
  the address to give an ambulance, who does what.
- Know the **weather policy** for anything outdoors, and who calls it.

## Templates

Downloadable, editable, and generated locally — no third-party service involved:

- **Officer transition checklist** — what the outgoing board hands over, and by when.
- **Event risk assessment** — hazards, controls, emergency plan, sign-off.
- **Recruiting table kit** — what to bring, what to say, how to capture interest.
- **Meeting agenda** — a standing agenda that keeps a club meeting to its hour.
