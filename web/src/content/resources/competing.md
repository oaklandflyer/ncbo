There is no NCBO season yet, and NCBO does not run competitions today. If you want to
compete now, you compete through an existing federation — the same way every collegiate
lifter currently does. This page describes the two established pathways factually. It does
not recommend one over the other.

## The collegiate route: NPC

The established collegiate route in the United States is the **NPC Teen, Collegiate &
Masters National Championships**, run under the National Physique Committee.

### Collegiate eligibility

To enter the collegiate division, a competitor must be **taking college courses during that
calendar year** and must **not be over 25 years of age**. (The teen division is a different
category with its own age limits.)

### You need an NPC card, bought in advance

**NPC cards are not available at the contest.** You must hold your card before the event.
It can be purchased through the entry form or at
[npcregistration.com](https://npcregistration.com).

This catches people out every year. A membership bought the week before is a membership; a
membership you intended to buy at the door is a wasted trip.

### Divisions

The NPC lists ten divisions:

- Men's Bodybuilding
- Men's Physique
- Classic Physique
- Women's Bodybuilding
- Women's Physique
- Figure
- Bikini
- Fitness
- Wellness
- Fit Model

Divisions are judged on different criteria, and the conditioning expected in one is not the
conditioning expected in another. Read the current division criteria before choosing, and
ask your club — someone who has stood on a stage will save you a season of guessing.

## The drug-tested pathway: natural federations

Drug-tested federations are a **separate pathway**, not a subdivision of the one above. If
you intend to compete drug-free, or you are a university athlete subject to your school's
or the NCAA's testing rules, this distinction matters before you enter anything.

The **World Natural Bodybuilding Federation (WNBF)** is one such federation. Its published
requirements:

- Athletes must be **10 years free** of prescription and pharmaceutical hormones on the
  banned substance list before their first WNBF competition.
- Athletes must be **2 years free** of over-the-counter hormones, pro-hormones and other
  listed banned substances or their chemical counterparts.
- Competitors are **polygraph tested and provide a urine sample** before competition.
- Since January 2022, a urine or polygraph failure for anabolic-androgenic steroids, growth
  hormones, peptides or SARMs carries a **lifetime ban** from INBF/WNBF membership.

Other drug-tested federations exist with their own rules and their own drug-free windows.
The windows are not interchangeable: being eligible for one federation tells you nothing
about your eligibility for another. Read the rulebook of the specific federation whose show
you intend to enter.

> **If you are a varsity athlete**, your athletic department's rules and your sport's
> anti-doping programme apply to you regardless of what any bodybuilding federation permits.
> Check with your compliance office before you enter anything or take any supplement. See
> also the supplement section of the [prep guide](/resources/prep).

## What we deliberately do not publish here

**Entry fees, contest dates and venues.** These change every season and vary by promoter.
Publishing a fee we read once would mean publishing a number that goes stale silently, and
somebody would budget against it. Get them from the current promoter page for the specific
show, on the day you enter.

## What we could not verify

We hold this site to publishing only what we can trace to a source. Two things we could not
confirm, and therefore do not state:

- **Whether the Teen and Collegiate divisions carry IFBB Professional League qualification.**
  The event awards pro cards subject to NPC and IFBB Pro League rules, and reporting on the
  event indicates that Masters divisions from 35 upward are eligible. We could not confirm
  from a primary source what applies to Teen and Collegiate winners. If pro qualification
  matters to your plans, ask the promoter directly before entering.
- **Certain WNBF specifics** sometimes quoted second-hand — including a shorter drug-free
  window for new amateur competitors, and the analytical thresholds used in urinalysis. The
  requirements listed above are the ones we could confirm from the federation's own policy
  page.
