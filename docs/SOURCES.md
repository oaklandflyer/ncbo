# Sources

Every external claim published on the hub, with the source it came from and the
date it was checked. Re-verify before relying on any of it — especially the
federation and helpline entries, which change.

**All entries accessed 2026-08-14.**

## Verification method, and its limit

The build environment can run web searches but **cannot fetch arbitrary pages
directly** (its egress proxy blocks them). So each claim below was corroborated
across independent search results reporting the same source, rather than by
opening the primary document. That is weaker than reading the source, and it is
why anything that did not corroborate cleanly was cut rather than published with
a hedge. See "Cut for lack of verification" at the end.

## Peer-reviewed literature

**Helms ER, Aragon AA, Fitschen PJ. Evidence-based recommendations for natural
bodybuilding contest preparation: nutrition and supplementation.** *Journal of the
International Society of Sports Nutrition.* 2014;11:20.
doi:[10.1186/1550-2783-11-20](https://doi.org/10.1186/1550-2783-11-20) ·
[PMC4033492](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4033492/)

Used on `/resources/prep` and `/resources/health` for:
- Weight loss of ~0.5–1% of bodyweight per week to maximise muscle retention
- Protein 2.3–3.1 g/kg of **lean body mass** per day, higher for leaner athletes
  and larger deficits
- Fat 15–30% of calories, carbohydrate the remainder
- 3–6 meals daily; ~0.4–0.5 g/kg bodyweight of protein around training
- Creatine monohydrate, caffeine and beta-alanine as the supplements with
  supporting evidence
- Carbohydrate loading as theoretically motivated but understudied
- Dehydration/water manipulation as a practice that **can be dangerous and may
  not improve appearance** — the basis of the content-policy exclusion
- The recommendation that competitors have access to appropriate mental health
  professionals

**Jäger R, et al. International Society of Sports Nutrition Position Stand:
protein and exercise.** *JISSN.* 2017;14:20.
doi:[10.1186/s12970-017-0177-8](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12970-017-0177-8) ·
[PMC5477153](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5477153/)

Used on `/resources/prep` for: 1.4–2.0 g/kg bodyweight/day for most exercising
individuals; 0.25 g/kg per dose or 20–40 g absolute; doses distributed every 3–4
hours.

**Mountjoy M, et al. 2023 International Olympic Committee's (IOC) consensus
statement on Relative Energy Deficiency in Sport (REDs).** *British Journal of
Sports Medicine.* 2023;57(17):1073–1097.
doi:[10.1136/bjsports-2023-106994](https://doi.org/10.1136/bjsports-2023-106994) ·
[bjsm.bmj.com/content/57/17/1073](https://bjsm.bmj.com/content/57/17/1073)

Used on `/resources/health` for the definition of REDs, low energy availability as
the primary cause, LEA as a spectrum from adaptable to problematic, its
applicability to male and female athletes, and the health and performance
consequence lists.

> **Citation note.** The brief supplied this as "Br J Sports Med
> 2024;57:1073–1098". Corroborated details give **2023**, volume 57, issue 17,
> pages **1073–1097**. The site publishes the corroborated form.

## Anti-doping and supplements

**U.S. Anti-Doping Agency — Supplement Connect / Third-Party Testing Guidance**
[usada.org](https://www.usada.org/substances/supplement-connect/reduce-risk-testing-positive-experiencing-adverse-health-effects/) ·
[third-party testing guidance](https://www.usada.org/substances/supplement-connect/reduce-risk-testing-positive-experiencing-adverse-health-effects/third-party-testing-guidance/)

Used on `/resources/prep` for: use only third-party certified products; USADA
recognises NSF Certified for Sport; a credible programme is accredited to **ISO
17065** with analysis in an ISO 17025 laboratory whose scope covers dietary
supplements; certification **"significantly reduces, but does not necessarily
eliminate"** the chance of testing positive; verify lot numbers.

## Federations

**NPC Teen, Collegiate & Masters National Championships — promoter general
information** [garyudit.com](https://garyudit.com/new/contest/npc-teen-collegiate-and-masters-national-championships-general-information/)
and **NPC News Online** [event listing](https://npcnewsonline.com/schedule_event/2026-npc-teen-collegiate-masters-national-championships/) ·
[rules](https://npcnewsonline.com/rules/)

Used on `/resources/competing` for: collegiate competitors must be taking college
courses during that calendar year and not be over 25; NPC cards are not available
at the contest and must be held in advance, purchasable via the entry form or
npcregistration.com.

**National Physique Committee — divisions**
[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Physique_Committee)

Used for the division list. Note this gives **ten** divisions including Fit Model;
the brief listed nine. The site publishes the ten-division list.

**World Natural Bodybuilding Federation — anti-doping / drug testing policies**
[worldnaturalbb.com](https://worldnaturalbb.com/competitor-info/anti-doping-policies/)

Used on `/resources/competing` for: 10 years free of prescription/pharmaceutical
hormones on the banned list; 2 years free of over-the-counter hormones,
pro-hormones and listed substances or their chemical counterparts; polygraph plus
urinalysis before competition; lifetime ban since January 2022 for anabolic
steroid, growth hormone, peptide or SARM failures.

## Health support services

**National Alliance for Eating Disorders** —
[allianceforeatingdisorders.com](https://www.allianceforeatingdisorders.com/) ·
[contact](https://www.allianceforeatingdisorders.com/contact/)
Helpline 866-662-1235, Monday–Friday 9am–7pm ET, staffed by licensed clinicians.

**ANAD (National Association of Anorexia Nervosa and Associated Disorders)** —
[anad.org](https://anad.org/) ·
[helpline](https://anad.org/eating-disorder-helpline/) ·
[athletes and eating disorders](https://anad.org/get-informed/athletes-and-eating-disorders/)
Helpline 888-375-7767, Monday–Friday 9am–9pm CT. Athlete-specific material.

**NEDA helpline — discontinued.** Corroborated by
[NPR](https://www.npr.org/sections/health-shots/2023/05/31/1179244569/national-eating-disorders-association-phases-out-human-helpline-pivots-to-chatbo)
and [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/eating-disorder-helpline-chatbot-disabled/):
the human-staffed helpline was phased out in 2023 and its chatbot replacement was
taken down after giving harmful advice. Published on `/resources/health` as a
warning, never as a referral.

## Cut for lack of verification

Claims from the brief that could not be corroborated, and are therefore **not
published**:

1. **"Teen and Collegiate competitors do not qualify for IFBB Professional League
   status."** Search results indicate the event awards pro cards subject to NPC
   and IFBB Pro League rules, with Masters divisions from 35 upward eligible, but
   nothing confirmed the Teen/Collegiate position. `/resources/competing` says
   explicitly that this could not be verified and to ask the promoter.
2. **WNBF: "new amateur competitors must be 5 years free of pro-hormones and
   SARMs."** Not found in the corroborated policy text.
3. **WNBF urinalysis specifics — T/E ratio flagged above 4:1, GC/MS confirmation.**
   Not corroborated. The site states polygraph and urinalysis without thresholds.
4. **Entry fees, contest dates and venues.** Deliberately never published, per the
   brief.
