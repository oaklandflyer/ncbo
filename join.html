What it costs

Dues, not
memberships.

There is no separate NCBO fee. You pay dues to your club, and your club sets that number together with the NCBO executive team — so it stays in range for students at your school rather than being handed down from above.

Your club keeps the majority. That’s what pays for their events, gear and travel. The percentage that comes to NCBO funds the things no single club can run alone: sanctioned competition, the season calendar, coaching and pro access, and the playbook every new club starts from.
