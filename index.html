What We Are

Not another
open show.
A real
collegiate sport.

NCBO gives competitive bodybuilding a legitimate home on campus. We organize student athletes into school-affiliated clubs, run them across a season, and put schools head-to-head — building toward a national collegiate championship.

At a traditional open show, "collegiate" is just an eligibility checkbox. NCBO adds the part that's been missing: a club at your school, a season to train for, school-vs-school competition, and a community that lasts all year — not one show and done.

The Roadmap

Where we are,
and where
this goes.

We're honest about the stage we're at. NCBO is early — and that's the point. This is the ground floor of a national sport, built phase by phase.

Phase 01We are here

Foundation

Assembling the board, standing up clubs, and building the infrastructure and first sponsor partnerships.

Phase 02

First Event

Our first sanctioned NCBO competition — the first time clubs meet on stage. Targeted within the year.

Phase 03

Scaling the Circuit

Clubs organize into conferences and a repeatable, school-vs-school season takes shape across regions.

Phase 04

National Championship

The season culminates where it's always been headed: a national collegiate bodybuilding championship.

The Club Model

Built on
clubs.

Every NCBO school runs a club. Clubs compete as teams while athletes chase individual titles. School spirit meets the stage — and it's how a national sport actually gets built.

Community

A year-round crew that trains, preps, and competes together. Not one show a year — a season, and a team behind you for all of it.

Coaching

Guidance from prep to posing, so first-timers have a real path to the stage instead of figuring it out alone.

Sponsor Product

Brand partners supply the clubs — supplements, apparel, and gear — so members get real value from day one.

The Pathway

A clear beginner-to-competitor track. Walk in brand new, walk out a competitor with a stage under your belt.
