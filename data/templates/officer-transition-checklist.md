# Officer Transition Checklist

Club: ______________________  Academic year: ____________

Hand this over in person, with the outgoing and incoming officer both present. A transition
done over text loses the club a semester.

## Accounts and access
- [ ] Club email account — password reset, incoming officer confirmed as owner
- [ ] Social media accounts — access transferred, old personal logins removed
- [ ] Shared drive / documents — ownership moved off a graduating student's account
- [ ] University club portal login and registration status
- [ ] Any subscriptions or paid tools, with renewal dates

## Documents on file
- [ ] Current bylaws
- [ ] Current roster
- [ ] Budget and financial records for the year
- [ ] Equipment inventory, with condition noted
- [ ] Signed waivers and emergency contacts, stored where an officer can reach them

## Compliance dates
- [ ] Annual club registration / recertification deadline: ____________
- [ ] Officer training requirement and date: ____________
- [ ] CPR/AED/First Aid certifications — who holds them, and expiry dates:
      Name: ______________ Expires: ________
      Name: ______________ Expires: ________
- [ ] Facility reservation windows and lead times for each space used

## Relationships
- [ ] Faculty or staff advisor — introduced in person
- [ ] Campus recreation contact
- [ ] Student activities / conduct contact
- [ ] NCBO chapter contact

## Knowledge transfer
- [ ] What worked this year, in writing
- [ ] What failed this year, in writing — more useful than the above
- [ ] Recruiting calendar: when the club actually gains members
- [ ] Standing meeting time and room, and how it was booked
