Who We Are

Building a real
collegiate sport.

NCBO is the governing body for collegiate bodybuilding. We sit above independently-run student clubs and give them a season, competition, coaching, and a national network.

Our mission

Clubs.
Competition.
Community.

We organize student athletes into school-affiliated clubs, run them across a season, and put schools head-to-head. At a traditional open show, "collegiate" is just an eligibility checkbox. NCBO adds the part that's been missing — a club at your school, a season to train for, and people who show up year-round.

Our job is the unglamorous infrastructure: the playbook, the coaching access, the legitimacy on campus, and the network that ties it all together. The clubs stay yours. We make them bigger than themselves.

The model

We sit above the clubs,
not in place of them.

Think of NCBO as the layer that an individual club can't build on its own — like a governing body sitting over independently-run programs.
