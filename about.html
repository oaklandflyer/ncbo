About

Who we are.

A free network of collegiate bodybuilding and fitness club boards, run entirely by students.

To be clear

What NCBO is not.

01

Not a governing body

We do not sanction, rank, or approve anything. Your club answers to your school, not to us.

02

Not a membership you buy

There are no dues, no fees, and no individual memberships. Boards join, and joining is free.

03

Not a promoter

We do not run shows or sell tickets. Clubs compete wherever they choose to compete.

04

Not a staffed organization

No employees and no office. Students who run clubs, helping other students who run clubs.
